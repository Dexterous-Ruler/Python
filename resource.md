# Learning Strategy

For every topic you study, use ChatGPT to identify gaps in your knowledge and build a complete roadmap.

After finishing a topic, ask ChatGPT:

* What additional concepts should I learn?
* What prerequisites might I be missing?
* What related topics should I study?
* What advanced topics build upon this topic?
* What interview questions are commonly asked?
* What practical applications use this topic?
* What implementation details should I know?

Do not move to the next topic until you can:

1. Explain the concept in your own words.
2. Solve related problems.
3. Implement the algorithm from scratch.
4. Build a small project using it.
5. Verify your understanding with ChatGPT.

# Final Rule

Never assume you have fully mastered a topic.

For every topic:

1. Learn the theory.
2. Learn the mathematics.
3. Implement it from scratch.
4. Implement it using industry-standard libraries.
5. Solve practice problems.
6. Build at least one project.
7. Ask ChatGPT to identify gaps in your knowledge.
8. Verify your understanding through questioning and debugging exercises.
9. Only then move to the next topic.
